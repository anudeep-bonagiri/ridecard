# Frio River and Garner

## The plan
The ride starts in Boerne, covering 161 miles and taking approximately 4.5 hours. This trip offers the curves you requested and includes a hiking opportunity, with today’s clear weather and a high of 84 degrees.

## Road notes
- Route: FM 470 west to Utopia, FM 1050 to Garner, US-83 north to Leakey, RR 337 east through Vanderpool and Medina.
- Full day. RR 337 between Leakey and Medina is tight and has livestock and loose gravel in corners. Fuel up in Leakey.
- Load the GPX before you leave. The route is baked in, so it works with no signal.

## Get off the bike
- **Garner State Park** (75 min on foot): Hike Old Baldy for the view over the Frio, then cool off at the river.
- **Leakey** (30 min on foot): Fuel, food, stretch.
